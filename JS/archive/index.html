Equinox cassette archive

Original surviving artwork and cassette scans from Equinox. The material documents both the 1987 demo Kidkkus and the 1988 demo Zzzzzzyzzzzzz, including its hand-made cover, track-list inlay, credits and cassette sides.

  • 1987Kidkkus — original cover artwork
  • 1988Zzzzzzyzzzzzz — cover, inlay and cassette scans
  • CreditsJohn: vocals/guitar; production and mixing documented on the 1988 inlay

Xtortex — Twisted

Surviving original artwork for the Xtortex demo Twisted. The fold-out inlay preserves the cover, track listing, recording notes, credits and band information, while the separate cover scan shows the hand-drawn skull artwork and original contact panel in greater detail.

  • ReleaseTwisted — demo cassette, 1990
  • JohnJohn Swahn — guitars
  • TracksIntro/Sacrifice, Fatal Chaos, Humanity of Society, Fascist Rise/Outro
  • SessionRecorded and mixed in November 1990; the original inlay credits Henrik Lindahl as engineer and David Giese for special-effects editing

  • JohnVocals; text & music credited to J. Swahn

2003 · studio and live archive

The Unkinds archive

Supplied original artwork for two 2003 Unkinds releases: the studio release Almost Human and the live release Live at Fellini 2003. Together they document the band in Uppsala during the same year, with both a formal release package and a separate live document from Fellini.

  • Studio releaseAlmost Human — original front cover and spread artwork
  • Live releaseLive at Fellini 2003 — original front and back artwork, Uppsala · 20 Feb 2003
  • JohnVocals on Almost Human; credited in the supplied artwork alongside Henrik Sundin, Anders Björk, Johan Asp and Andreas Engman
  • SessionAlmost Human was recorded and mixed at Ear Studio by Carl Wikman in 2003 according to the supplied sleeve art

Treebeard archive

A substantial Treebeard cover-art collection drawn from the supplied archive. The current material documents the main release run from The Eldest in 2003 through Anguish On Parade, Not for Sale, May Contain Small Bones, Bulletin Board and Admiration, alongside additional later Treebeard cover images that can be tied to specific releases as more source information is added.

  • 2003The Eldest — original supplied cover/back artwork
  • 2004–2006Anguish On Parade, Not for Sale and May Contain Small Bones
  • 2008Bulletin Board and Admiration with detailed text-heavy back-cover artwork
  • AdditionalThree further Treebeard cover images are now preserved in the archive and can be matched to later releases as more documentation arrives

Aphophis archive

The largest original-artwork collection currently on the site. The supplied material now begins with Principle of Evil in 2008, continues through The Books of Overthrowing Apep and Sarcophagus, the 2012–2016 period and into a dense run of 2024–2026 releases. Several titles include front, back and inside artwork with track lists, production notes and musician credits.

  • 2008Principle of Evil — front and back artwork; produced by John Swahn, A SwahnSong Production 2008
  • 2009–2016The Books of Overthrowing Apep, Sarcophagus, Hieroglyphs, Symphony for the Devil and The Spherical Waltz
  • 2024–2025Exit Space Left, Exit Space Leftovers, Through The Hourglass, Dynasties and The Aphophis Universe, Phase One
  • 2026Chronophobia — front, back, inner artwork and full spread
  • ChronophobiaProduced and mixed by John Swahn; recorded at Electric Crypt. John is credited with vocals, guitar & programming and Janne Eriksson with guitars.
  • WritingThe Chronophobia artwork credits all songs to John Swahn except Pipeline, written by Martin L. Gore.

Desecrate archive

Surviving material from Desecrate's original Uppsala period and the later return to the songs. The collection includes demo artwork and inlays, cassette photographs, a band-photo collage, contemporary fanzine material, a Storvreta show flyer and the 2026 Second Death artwork.

  • 1988We Only Make Jokes... We Made You — cover art, cassette material and contemporary press
  • 1989Arranger of Disorder and Lonely Disgrace — original artwork and inlay material
  • SessionArranger of Disorder recorded at Musikhuset, Enköping, 27–28 May 1989; John S. Swahn credited on vocals
  • 2026Second Death reconnects the current album with the band's surviving early material
