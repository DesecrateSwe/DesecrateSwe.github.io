About

A life in music.

John Swahn is a Swedish vocalist, musician and songwriter whose documented recordings reach back to 1987 and Uppsala's underground scene. Across more than three decades, the archive spans bands, demos, cassettes, live recordings, songwriting, performance, production and later studio projects.

This site is built as a growing personal music archive: a place to collect the projects, releases, recordings, photographs, flyers and stories that form John Swahn's musical history.

The supplied archive already maps at least 40 recording folders from 1987 to 2026, now supplemented by original NFO data for 20 preserved releases. John’s Discogs profile currently documents 35 credits across vocals, writing and arrangement, instruments and performance, production and technical work. Encyclopaedia Metallum also documents his early metal-era roles: vocals in Desecrate, vocals and guitars in Equinox, guitars in Xtortex, plus production and mixing work on Equinox’s 1988 demo.

Journey

The story, at a glance.

The homepage now carries the overview. Detailed project histories, releases, songs and original-media galleries live on their own pages.

1987

The earliest documented recordings

The current archive begins with Equinox's Kidkkus and Twilight's Rock You. On Kidkkus, John is credited with vocals, guitars, bass and drums.

1988

Equinox and the beginning of Desecrate

Equinox records the demo Zzzzzzyzzzzzz, with John credited for vocals and guitars as well as production and mixing. In the same year Desecrate emerges in Uppsala and records We Only Make Jokes... We Made You, with John on vocals.

1989

Desecrate intensifies

Arranger of Disorder, Lonely Disgrace and a surviving live recording document the band's active period. The original Arranger of Disorder inlay records sessions at Musikhuset in Enköping on 27–28 May 1989, with John S. Swahn on vocals; production is credited to Kari and the mix to Kari and Desecrate.

1991

Big November

Wonders Of Devotion I & II appears in the archive, while Mirrors Do The Talkin' is documented on the Nyby Fritidsgård compilation.

2003

The Unkinds and Treebeard

The Unkinds are represented by Almost Human and a live recording from Fellini in Uppsala. The same year begins the documented Treebeard run with The Eldest.

2008–16

Aphophis

Aphophis becomes a major part of the archive, beginning with Principle of Evil and continuing through Sarcophagus, Hieroglyphs, Symphony for the Devil and The Spherical Waltz.
