Aphophis archive

The largest original-artwork collection currently on the site. The supplied material now begins with Principle of Evil in 2008, continues through The Books of Overthrowing Apep and Sarcophagus, the 2012–2016 period and into a dense run of 2024–2026 releases. Several titles include front, back and inside artwork with track lists, production notes and musician credits.

  • 2008Principle of Evil — front and back artwork; produced by John Swahn, A SwahnSong Production 2008
  • 2009–2016The Books of Overthrowing Apep, Sarcophagus, Hieroglyphs, Symphony for the Devil and The Spherical Waltz
  • 2024–2025Exit Space Left, Exit Space Leftovers, Through The Hourglass, Dynasties and The Aphophis Universe, Phase One
  • 2026Chronophobia — front, back, inner artwork and full spread
  • ChronophobiaProduced and mixed by John Swahn; recorded at Electric Crypt. John is credited with vocals, guitar & programming and Janne Eriksson with guitars.
  • WritingThe Chronophobia artwork credits all songs to John Swahn except Pipeline, written by Martin L. Gore.
