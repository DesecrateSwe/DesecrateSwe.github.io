Equinox

The earliest project currently represented in the archive. On the 1987 demo Kidkkus , John is credited with vocals, guitars, bass and drums. The surviving 1988 Zzzzzzyzzzzzz inlay credits John on vocals/guitar and states that the demo was produced and mixed by John Eric Olof Swahn.
