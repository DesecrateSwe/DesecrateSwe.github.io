THE FULL STORY · 1987–2026

DESECRATE

The history of the band, reconstructed from the surviving demos, contemporary fanzines and the memories of the people who were there.

1987
BEFORE DESECRATE

TWILIGHT, EQUINOX & THE UPPSALA SCENE

John Swahn, Rickard Ceder and Jakob Bergström played together in Twilight during 1987–88 and reached the final of an Uppsala talent competition in 1987. John and Jakob also had the thrash project Equinox, which recorded the demo Kidkkus!.

1988
THE BEGINNING

CEDER WANTED IT HEAVIER

Rickard Ceder wanted to make harder music and started working with Jakob “Kobben” Bergström on what became Desecrate's first demo. The earliest songs were put together on a Portastudio at Nyby Fritidsgård. Neither Ceder nor Kobben felt comfortable singing, so John Swahn was asked to handle the vocals.

The first three-piece was Ceder on guitar and bass, Kobben on drums and John on vocals. Their self-produced tape, We Only Make Jokes...We Made You, was initially made largely for fun but became the starting point for the band.

25 NOV
1988
FIRST SHOW · STORVRETA FRITIDSGÅRD

THE GIG THAT ALMOST DIDN'T HAPPEN

For live shows the trio expanded with Måns Magnusson on guitar and Mårten Korkman on bass. The first Desecrate concert took place at Storvreta Fritidsgård on 25 November 1988.

The show nearly got cancelled after one of the organisers saw a band member drinking a folköl (low-alcohol beer) at lunchtime. After major protests from the audience, the concert was allowed to go ahead.

1988–89
THE LOST DEMO

SECOND DEATH

A second demo was started with the songs Second Death and Possessor of Life, but the recording was accidentally erased during the final mix. The songs survived through live recordings. Desecrate also played support to Strebers at Ungdomens Hus in Uppsala.

MAY
1989
ARRANGER OF DISORDER

THE FIRST ENKÖPING SESSION

Mårten Korkman had other bands and left. Dave Janney, previously in R.I.P. — a band that had supported Desecrate — replaced him on bass. Arranger of Disorder was recorded at Musikhuset in Enköping on 27–28 May 1989, with Måns Magnusson on lead guitar.

A contemporary fanzine interview says the band had played around ten shows in the Uppsala area and sold approximately 70 copies of the demo. At the time they described their newer material as somewhat more technical, with a slight crossover influence.

DEC
1989
LONELY DISGRACE

ONE MORE LINEUP, ONE MORE TAPE

By the third demo Dave had moved from bass to guitar, replacing Måns, while Jens “Jenka” Åberg — also previously in R.I.P. — came in on bass. Lonely Disgrace was recorded at Musikhuset in Enköping on 3 and 6 December 1989.

1990
THE END OF THE FIRST ERA

THE LAST SHOW

Desecrate played several shows supporting Fallen Angel from Örebro. Differences over how seriously to pursue the band and over musical direction eventually led to the split. The final concert of the original era took place at the old fire station in Knivsta.

2024
→26
SECOND DEATH

THE SONGS RETURN

In 2024 John Swahn made a new version of Disorder with his band Aphophis for the demo Exit Space Left. That experiment led to the idea of rebuilding the old Desecrate material.

The original recordings were separated into individual instruments, with AI-assisted restoration used to improve the source audio and help give the material a modern treatment. The result became Desecrate's 2026 full-length album Second Death — old songs given a second life decades after the band disappeared.

SURVIVING LIVE RECORDINGS

THE LOST SECOND DEATH

The original studio recording of Second Death and Possessor of Life was erased during the final mix. These live recordings preserve the songs from the original era. Second Death, Angels Fuck and Scooby Dooaaauöö II were recorded at Desecrate's first show at Storvreta Fritidsgård on 25 November 1988. Possessor of Life and Scooby Dooaaauöö III were recorded at Nybygården; the date is currently unknown.
