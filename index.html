THE STORY
1987John Swahn, Rickard Ceder and Jakob Bergström played together in Twilight and reached the final of an Uppsala talent competition. John and Jakob also had the tongue-in-cheek thrash project Equinox.
1988Rickard Ceder wanted to play heavier music. Together with Jakob Bergström he began the first Desecrate demo at Nyby Fritidsgård. John Swahn was brought in on vocals.
1989Arranger Of Disorder and Lonely Disgrace were recorded at Musikhuset in Enköping.
1990After several live shows, including support slots for Fallen Angel, differences over musical direction and ambition led to the band splitting up.
2026The old songs return on Desecrate's first full-length album, Second Death.

MEMBERS & HISTORY

Desecrate was formed by guitarist Rickard Ceder. During the band's original late-eighties period the lineup changed several times between the first demo, the live lineup and the two 1989 recordings.

1988
THE FIRST LINEUP

WE ONLY MAKE JOKES...WE MADE YOU

The original three-piece consisted of founder Rickard Ceder, drummer Jakob “Kobben” Bergström and vocalist John Swahn. This was the lineup behind Desecrate's first self-produced demo.

Rickard Ceder · guitar / bassJakob “Kobben” Bergström · drumsJohn Swahn · vocals
LIVE
THE SECOND LINEUP

EXPANDED FOR THE STAGE

The next incarnation existed as a live lineup. Måns Magnusson joined on guitar and Mårten Korkman on bass, expanding the original trio into a five-piece.

John Swahn · vocalsRickard Ceder · guitarMåns Magnusson · guitarMårten Korkman · bassJakob Bergström · drums
1989
SECOND DEMO

ARRANGER OF DISORDER

For Arranger Of Disorder, Dave Janney replaced Mårten Korkman on bass. The demo was recorded at Musikhuset in Enköping on 27–28 May 1989.

John Swahn · vocalsRickard Ceder · guitarMåns Magnusson · lead guitarDave Janney · bassJakob Bergström · drums
1989
THIRD DEMO

LONELY DISGRACE

The lineup changed again for the final documented demo of the original era. Jens “Jenka” Åberg came in on bass while Dave Janney moved to guitar. The tape was recorded in Enköping on 3 and 6 December 1989.

John Swahn · vocalsRickard Ceder · guitarDave Janney · guitarJens “Jenka” Åberg · bassJakob Bergström · drums
