FROM THE ARCHIVE
THE STORY · 1987 → 2026

FROM NYBY
TO SECOND DEATH.

Desecrate grew out of Uppsala's late-eighties underground. Rickard Ceder wanted to make harder music and began working with Jakob “Kobben” Bergström on the first Portastudio recordings. John Swahn was brought in on vocals.

1988
FIRST DEMO & FIRST SHOW

We Only Make Jokes...We Made You. First concert at Storvreta Fritidsgård on 25 November.

1989
TWO ENKÖPING DEMOS

Arranger of Disorder in May and Lonely Disgrace in December.

1990
THE LAST SHOW

Differences over direction led to the split. Final show at the old fire station in Knivsta.

2026
THE SONGS RETURN

The old material is rebuilt as the full-length album Second Death.
